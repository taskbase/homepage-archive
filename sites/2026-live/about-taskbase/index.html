About Us

At Taskbase we’re lifelong learners

Human-centric Learning

Empowering humanity through learning

At Taskbase, we believe that learning is deeply human.

It’s driven by curiosity, shaped by experience, and sustained by the relationships between people. Yet in the rush to make education more efficient, the human experience can too easily be reduced to metrics and outputs.

We’re building technology to change that. Our tools are designed to understand the learner — not just their answers, but their intentions, their effort, and their growth over time. Because feedback is not just data; it’s dialogue.

We see education as a shared process of discovery. Every improvement to our platform comes from the same mindset we hope to foster in others: listening, questioning, learning. We’re also learners — and we want technology to keep that spirit alive in every classroom, platform, and conversation.

Samuel Portmann

CEO at Taskbase
