Have your sales reps walk into every meeting prepared, leave every call coached, and never lose a deal to admin again. Taskbase connects to your playbook, CRM, calendar, and call recordings to deliver real-time coaching, AI-prepared briefings, automated follow-through, and much more; so every rep performs at their best.

Core Benefits

Effortless Enablement for Sales Leads

Instead of manually managing training, prep, and follow-through, leaders set the objectives; and Taskbase coaches, prepares, and executes alongside every rep. Individually for the rep.

Skilled-up Reps — Real Coaching, Real Results

AI-powered feedback on every real call sharpens your reps' messaging, objection handling, and confidence — grounded in your playbook, delivered minutes after the call ends. No need for you to be in the room.

Reps Walk Into Every Meeting Prepared

Before every external meeting, reps receive an AI-generated briefing: deal context, full prior interaction history, stakeholder intel, and the right opener for the deal stage — straight from your playbook.

The Admin Work — Done For Them

Post-meeting follow-up drafts, CRM hygiene, business cases, and CS hand-offs are generated automatically. Your reps get 6+ hours back per week — to spend on what only humans can do: sell.

Visible Growth, Every Step of the Way

From ramp-up speed and skill mastery to meeting prep quality and follow-through, you see progress in motion — and trust that every rep is moving in the right direction.

Key Results

Effective Enablement for Sales Reps

On-demand Coaching

Real-time feedback and practice sessions after every call, so every rep improves between trainings — without scheduling a single in-person coaching session.

AI-Prepared Meetings

Every external meeting comes with a tailored briefing — deal stage, prior calls and notes, stakeholder context, skills to focus on, and the right opener from your playbook — delivered before the rep walks in.

Your Playbook in Every Conversation

With your methodology embedded, Taskbase reinforces the right messaging, qualification framework, and objection responses on every call — turning your playbook from a PDF into daily practice.

Automated Follow-Through

Follow-up emails, CRM updates, business cases, and CS hand-offs are drafted automatically from your call recordings and CRM data — so nothing slips through the cracks after a meeting. Zero friction for reps.

Proactive Nudges Coaching, briefings, and reminders delivered at the right place and the right time — never as a separate tab to open.

Embedded in Teams or Slack Coaching, prep, and execution happen inside the tools your reps already use — Teams, Slack, Gmail, HubSpot, and Salesforce.

Taskbase Skill Wallet A structured competency framework that maps every sales skill and sub-skill — so coaching is targeted, not generic.

Performance Analytics A continuous loop of measurement, insight, and adaptation across coaching, prep, and execution

Sales Skills

The Taskbase Skill Wallet for Sales Enablement gives you a structured view of the skills and sub-skills your reps need to master — defining what great looks like, and showing you exactly where each rep stands.
