FAQs

What about the AI Act?

We have the confirmation of a lawyer that this use case does not fall under any risk according to the EU AI Act. Therefore, we do not have to take special measures. This letter is part of the contract (Addendum 9)

What about data privacy?

Taskbase does not process any personal data. All the data is anonymised because the client can choose to only send the candidate’s ID from their database (or a completely random one) to Taskbase. On Taskbase’s database, the ID is stored with the results that cannot be connected to any personal data. Therefore, the data protection officers of our clients are usually very relaxed

Can we use the AI Coach if we already have trainings in place?

How much effort is it for us to implement?
